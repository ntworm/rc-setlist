RC Setlist 1.3.0 release notes

Release date: 2026-11-25 Supported version line: 1.3.x Also available in Portuguese: pt-BR/NOTAS-DA-VERSAO-1.3.0.md

Windows is the validated platform; macOS is experimental. This release is about the link between the main computer (the master) and the second computers (the slaves). The user guide describes each state and alert, and troubleshooting says what to do about each one.

New features

  • Link health. Stage Control, in Edit and in Show mode, /performance and

the Live panel show one row per paired computer with its state, OK, UNSTABLE or DOWN, the reason, the round-trip time (RTT), the drops in the last 5 minutes, the last drop and the last frame seen. The two computers exchange heartbeats in both directions and measure the link.

  • Warm-up, recovery and oscillation hold. A new link stays UNSTABLE for

a 20 s warm-up. After a drop, a lost heartbeat (> 1.5 s) or high latency (RTT p95 > 150 ms), it needs 60 s without trouble to return to OK. A link that drops 3 or more times in 5 min stays UNSTABLE for 5 min.

  • Acknowledge. When an UNSTABLE link has calmed down, Acknowledge on

Stage Control or in the Live panel shortens the clean window to 10 s on both computers. It never skips the warm-up or the 5 min hold.

  • Link-down alarm. When a link goes DOWN (no frame for 3 s, the socket

closed, or Live or OSC unavailable on either computer), a full-screen red alarm opens on Stage Control and /performance. Dismiss alarm closes it and keeps a red LINK DOWN badge above the transport dock. Stop stays reachable above the alarm.

  • Status dot. The second-computer dot on Stage Control follows the measured

state: green for OK, amber for UNSTABLE, red for DOWN, DIFFERENT VERSIONS or CERTIFICATE CHANGED.

  • Confirmed commands. The slave confirms every command the master sends,

and the master resends it every 150 ms until its deadline. Stage Control shows a 3 s tick per computer: Pending, Executed or Failed.

  • Command alerts. A command that misses its deadline or fails on the slave

shows SLAVE DID NOT EXECUTE: <command> on Stage Control and in the Live panel. A confirmation that arrives after the deadline changes it to Executed late: <command>.

  • Safety resend for Stop and Panic. While the master stays stopped or in

panic, Stop and Panic do not expire: they are resent across reconnects. If the slave has not confirmed within 1 s, SLAVE STILL NOT STOPPED shows until it does. A delayed Play behind a newer Stop or Panic is discarded and never runs out of order.

  • Stopped playhead follows the master. When both computers are stopped and

the slave is more than half a beat away for three targets in a row, the slave moves to the master's marker, or to the exact beat between markers. This also happens within 2 s of a reconnect.

  • Out of step. A playhead mismatch that lasts 3 s outside a pending

quantized jump or an active loop shows UNSTABLE with the reason Out of step on both computers.

  • Slave modes. On a slave, the Second computer card of the Network

tab has Slave mode with three buttons: - Follow all (default): the slave runs the master's commands and corrects its playhead while playing and while stopped. - Follow transport: the slave runs the master's commands and corrects its playhead while playing; stopped at another beat, it only alerts. - Independent: the master sends no commands to that slave and raises no alert for it, and the slave refuses any that arrive. The master shows an Independent tag beside that computer.

The choice is kept after a restart. A slave that ran a 1.3.0 test build with the Auto-correct playhead on slave checkbox keeps its choice: checked becomes Follow all, unchecked becomes Follow transport.

  • Slave navigation lock. In Follow all and Follow transport, while

the link to the master is up, Stage Control on the slave cannot play, jump, count in, or change the metronome, the quantization or the pre-roll. A banner says the slave is following the main computer. Stop, Panic and Refresh stay available and go up to the master, so a Stop on the slave stops the whole show. In Independent, Stage Control on the slave runs on that computer only.

  • Link protocol check. Master and slave exchange a link protocol version.

A computer on another version shows DIFFERENT VERSIONS, and no command is relayed to it.

Changes

  • A phone on a slave no longer drives the whole show. In 1.2.1, play, jump,

metronome, quantization and count-in commands from a phone or the Live panel on a slave went up to the master and moved every computer. In 1.3.0, a slave in Follow all or Follow transport only sends Stop, Panic and Refresh up. To navigate on a slave on its own, choose Independent.

  • Network tab. The Second computer card sits above the Certificate

card.

Included from 1.2.1

Version 1.2.1 was tagged without a release page. Its fixes are part of 1.3.0:

  • Ready to pair shows an address that answers. When a slave has two network

interfaces on the same subnet, the announcement carries its LAN addresses and the master lists the first one that answers on the announced port.

  • Commands from a slave reach the master. With the master connected, the

commands a slave hands up are run by the master and sent to every slave; in 1.3.0 this applies to Stop, Panic and Refresh while the slave follows the master.

Upgrading

Nothing to migrate; the data folder and the pairings stay. Live refuses to install over an existing extension folder: remove RC Setlist in Live's extension settings first, then open the .ablx. Install 1.3.0 on the master and on every slave. Both computers must run the same version: when a 1.2.x computer is paired with a 1.3.0 computer, the 1.3.0 side shows DIFFERENT VERSIONS and no command passes between them. The RC Bridge Remote Script did not change, so you do not need to reinstall it.
